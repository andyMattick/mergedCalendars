Learned something here that isn't about this project's own code, and can't reach the team repo from this session?
Add one line below. The weekly run takes each line to the team's `LESSONS.md`, so the owner's other projects get it,
and empties this file. A line that waits more than 14 days fails this project's check.

One line a lesson: `- 2026-11-20 | what went wrong, with the numbers | what catches it now, or "reader only"`

Nothing from a real class or a real order: counts and findings only.
